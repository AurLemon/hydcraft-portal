HydCraft is a Minecraft community around building, railways, city planning, and server development. It combines long-running survival worlds with player-maintained documentation, transport systems, and shared infrastructure.

## Community Focus

The important part is whether players can keep leaving durable work inside the server. Buildings, routes, towns, map notes, and documents all become part of the community memory.

## Site Purpose

This portal collects server entries, public services, project notes, and timeline records. Detailed rules and references will remain in the Wiki and Docs.
