HydCraft, pronounced /ˈhaɪd.kræft/, is also known in Chinese as "氢气工艺", and is usually shortened to "Hyd" or "H2".

## Overview

HydCraft is a Minecraft server focused on city building and railway transportation. The server was founded on September 1, 2018, and currently runs on Forge 1.20.1. Its main gameplay mods include MTR and Create.

Why name a creative-mode server after a chemical element? The name originally came from a player's joke: "OnePlus phones have a system called Hydrogen OS, why not just call it that?" In November 2018, that offhand name became the server's official title, and it has remained in use ever since.

## This Site

This site is the central entry point for HydCraft. It organizes server information, public services, status monitoring, map links, and community resources. In the future, it will also gradually integrate player profiles, account linking, server status, and more community systems.

More detailed rules, tutorials, and references will continue to live in the Wiki and Docs. For a fuller server history, see the "Timeline" page.
