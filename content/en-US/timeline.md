This page records key HydCraft moments from the early server period to the current season. The first version stays short, and it can later expand by season, project, and community event.

## Early Stage

The server began as a small Minecraft world among friends, then gradually formed a stable player group, shared facilities, and maintenance habits.

## Current Direction

The current stage focuses on documentation, transport networks, building clusters, and site tooling. The portal acts as the entry point that connects the Wiki, Docs, status monitor, and future content pages.
